Our story

Sport should be easy to reach.

We're building one app that makes following, booking and playing sport simple.

The problem

Scores, news, courts and players, spread across five apps. Outdoor puts them in one.

Scores News Booking Videos Group chats

What we won't build

  • No feeds or follower counts.
  • No fantasy or betting.
  • No clickbait.

The long game

Built in three phases.

  1. 01

    Now · We're here

    Community

    One app to follow every sport, catch the day's news, book a court and find a game. The base for everything else.

  2. 02

    Next

    Infrastructure

    World-class places to play across India, and the tools to run them.

  3. 03

    Later

    Sports-tech

    Wearables, sensors and data that help every player, at every level, get better.

Questions

Good to know.

What is Outdoor?

An app for every sport. Follow your teams and tournaments, read the day's sport news in short cards, and book courts and turfs near you. Communities, for finding players and games, comes next.

When does the app launch?

Soon. Everyone on the waitlist hears first.

Which sports can I follow?

Cricket, football, tennis, F1 and chess today, with more being added. The daily news already covers many more sports.

I run a venue. How do I list it?

Write to team.outdoor.in@gmail.com and we'll get you set up.

What happens to my waitlist details?

We use your name and email only to tell you about Outdoor, and we delete them whenever you ask. More in our privacy policy.
